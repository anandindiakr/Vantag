The key differences: (1) Bazaar Suraksha is an Android phone app — it runs on your phone and stops if your phone battery dies or you close the app. Retail Nazar runs on cloud servers — it monitors 24/7 regardless of your phone status. "My cameras are CP Plus — will it really work?"Technical›

CP Plus is India's #1 selling CCTV brand. Prospect is rightfully cautious about compatibility.

"CP Plus is our most tested camera brand in India — specifically because it's the most common in Indian stores. We've integrated directly with CP Plus RTSP streams and tested extensively in Indian retail environments. We also support Hikvision, Dahua, and any ONVIF-compatible camera. Give me your CP Plus model number right now — I can tell you in 2 minutes if it's on our certified compatible list. In fact, let's just do the 3-day trial — you'll see it connect to your cameras live."

"The trial is the best proof — live connection to your CP Plus cameras, real alerts to your WhatsApp."

"My internet speed is not great — will alerts be delayed?"Technical›

Very valid concern for Indian retail stores — especially semi-urban locations.

"We built Retail Nazar specifically for Indian internet conditions — that was a conscious design decision. Our system measures your available bandwidth in real time and adjusts the alert payload accordingly: on good connections, you get a 10-second video clip; on slower connections, you still get the alert and a still image within 3 seconds. We also have a failover system — if WhatsApp delivery is delayed, we send an SMS backup. You will always get the alert. We've tested on connections as slow as 1 Mbps — the system still delivers."

"Your internet conditions are the exact conditions we designed for — the trial will prove it on your actual connection."

"Too many false alerts will distract my staff"Feature / UX›

Prospect has likely experienced poor rules-based systems with constant false alerts.

"This is actually our biggest design philosophy — we call it 'precision over recall'. Old CCTV systems used motion detection and generated hundreds of false alerts per day. Our AI is trained on actual shoplifting behavior patterns in Indian stores — not just any movement. We include a confidence score with every alert. You can set the sensitivity threshold — if you want only high-confidence alerts, we can configure that in your dashboard. Most of our users start at medium sensitivity during the trial and then fine-tune to their preference. The goal is that every WhatsApp alert you receive is worth looking at."

"The 3-day trial will show you the alert quality. If you get a false alert, tell us the scenario — we'll tune the sensitivity setting before you commit."

Retail Nazar supplements your guard — it gives them an alert too so they know exactly where to look. Your guard becomes more effective, not replaced."

Proven Closing Techniques for Indian Retail Stores

The 3-Day Trial Close For any hesitant prospect

"I'm not asking you to buy anything today. I'm asking you to try Retail Nazar on YOUR cameras for 3 days — completely free, no credit card, no commitment. If in 3 days you haven't received a single useful alert, don't pay us a rupee. But if your WhatsApp buzzes with a real incident from your store — something your staff would have missed — that's your proof. Can we set that up right now? It takes 15 minutes to connect to your cameras."

The ROI Close For price-sensitive owners

"Let me show you a quick calculation. Your Starter plan is ₹999/month — that's ₹33 per day. One incident of shoplifting at your [electronics/jewellery/pharmacy] store costs you how much? Even ₹2,000? That's 2 months of subscription. Most of our store owners tell us they catch 1–3 incidents per month they would have missed before. At ₹999, you only need to prevent ONE theft in a whole year to be in profit. The question isn't whether ₹999 is affordable — the question is whether the risk of NOT having it is affordable."

The WhatsApp Demo Close On-site visit or phone call

"Let me show you something — [open phone] — this is what you get on WhatsApp when an alert fires. [Show demo screenshot or video]. This came to the store owner's phone in under 3 seconds. He was at lunch, 2 kilometers away. He called his staff immediately. They caught the person. Now — if this happened at YOUR store right now and you were at lunch, how would you know? That's the gap we close. Shall we connect your cameras and make sure the next time this happens, you know within 3 seconds?"

The Urgency Close For stalling prospects

"I completely respect that you want to think it over. But here's a reality: every day without this system is a day your store is unprotected from exactly the incidents we've talked about. The 3-day trial is genuinely free — there's no financial risk in starting today. The only risk is continuing to leave your store exposed while you think about it. Can I be direct? What is the one thing that would need to be true for you to say yes today?"

The Multi-Store Close For chain store owners

"For a single store, our Starter plan at ₹999 gives you everything. But here's where it gets powerful — for your 3 stores, our Growth plan at ₹1,999 covers all three. That's ₹666 per store per month — about the cost of one extra hour of a security guard. And you get one unified dashboard showing all 3 stores' footfall, all alerts, everything in one view. Your accountant can run reports across all stores. Your manager can monitor remotely. That's the power of a real multi-tenant SaaS platform — which is something Bazaar Suraksha can't give you."

The Referral Plant Close After a positive meeting

"Before I go — since you're going to start the trial, I want to ask one thing. You know most store owners in this market, right? If Retail Nazar works for you the way I'm saying it will — and I'm confident it will — would you be open to introducing us to 2 or 3 other store owners you know? We have a referral program: for every store you refer that signs up, you get one month free. So essentially your subscription could become free if you refer 12 stores a year. That's our way of saying — we grow together."
