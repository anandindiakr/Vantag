Patent 1 — Indian Retail Concealment Detection

Application Reference: BGX-PAT-001 | Category: Computer Vision / AI

Method and System for Detecting Retail Theft Concealment Behaviors in Indian Retail Environments Using Context-Specific Computer Vision Models

Field of Invention
The present invention relates to computer vision-based behavioral detection systems, and more particularly to a method and system for detecting concealment-type shoplifting behaviors that are contextually specific to Indian retail environments, including concealment using Indian clothing items such as dupattas, sarees, kurtas, and Indian-style shopping bags, within retail stores.
Background of the Invention
Existing retail theft detection systems rely primarily on datasets and trained models developed in Western retail contexts. These systems fail to adequately detect concealment behaviors common in Indian retail environments where shoppers commonly wear draped garments (sarees, dupattas) and carry culturally specific bags (jhola bags, cloth bags) that create unique occlusion patterns different from Western concealment behaviors. Furthermore, Indian retail stores — particularly kirana stores, pharmacy stores, and jewellery shops — have unique spatial layouts including narrow aisles, floor-level shelving, and counter-based service that existing detection systems do not account for.

There is therefore a need for a method and system specifically trained and optimized for Indian retail concealment detection that accounts for: (a) Indian clothing concealment patterns, (b) Indian store spatial layouts, and (c) low-resolution camera feeds common in Indian retail (720p and below) with variable lighting conditions.
Summary of the Invention
The present invention provides a method and system for detecting retail theft concealment behaviors in Indian retail environments, comprising: (i) a computer vision model trained on an India-specific dataset of concealment behaviors, (ii) a preprocessing pipeline for normalizing low-resolution Indian CCTV camera feeds from Hikvision, Dahua, and CP Plus camera brands, (iii) a behavioral classification module that identifies concealment events specific to Indian retail contexts, and (iv) an alert generation module that transmits real-time notifications via the WhatsApp Business API within a predefined latency threshold.
Independent Claims
Claim 1 — Independent Method Claim ›
Claim 1: A computer-implemented method for detecting retail theft concealment behaviors in Indian retail environments, the method comprising:
  1. receiving, by a computing system, a live video stream from at least one surveillance camera installed at a retail premises in India, wherein the video stream is transmitted via Real-Time Streaming Protocol (RTSP) from a camera selected from a group consisting of Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus, and ONVIF-compatible cameras;
  2. preprocessing, by the computing system, the video stream frames through a normalization pipeline configured to compensate for low-resolution video artifacts, variable ambient lighting conditions, and camera distortions characteristic of sub-megapixel surveillance cameras commonly deployed in Indian retail stores;
  3. applying, by the computing system, a trained machine learning model to the preprocessed frames to detect one or more concealment behaviors from a set comprising: (a) placement of merchandise under or within draped Indian garments including sarees and dupattas, (b) insertion of merchandise into cloth carry bags of the type jhola, (c) sweeping of merchandise off shelving using body contact while maintaining visual obstruction using the body or garments, and (d) pocket-insertion gestures using deep-front pockets characteristic of kurta-style garments;
  4. generating, by the computing system, a concealment event record upon detection of any of the concealment behaviors, the event record including: a timestamp, a video clip of a predefined duration preceding and following the detected event, a confidence score, and a camera identifier;
  5. transmitting, by the computing system, the concealment event record as a real-time alert notification via the WhatsApp Business API to a pre-registered mobile device of the retail store owner or manager within a predefined latency threshold of not more than five seconds from the time of detection.
Claim 2 — Independent System Claim ›
Claim 2: A system for detecting retail theft concealment behaviors in Indian retail environments, the system comprising:
  1. a camera interface module configured to receive live video streams via RTSP protocol from Indian retail surveillance cameras;
  2. a preprocessing module configured to normalize video frames from cameras operating in resolution ranges from 480p to 1080p with frame rates between 10fps and 30fps;
  3. a machine learning inference engine comprising a convolutional neural network trained exclusively on an India-specific training dataset of concealment behaviors annotated in Indian retail store environments;
  4. an alert dispatch module configured to compose and transmit structured alerts including video evidence clips via the WhatsApp Business API; and
  5. a multi-tenant data isolation layer configured to logically separate detection data, alert histories, and model configurations across multiple retail store tenants.
Claim 3 — Dependent: Training Dataset ›
Claim 3 (dependent on Claim 2): The system of Claim 2, wherein the India-specific training dataset comprises annotated video sequences collected from Indian retail environments depicting concealment using Indian clothing types, Indian retail spatial configurations, and Indian ambient lighting conditions, wherein said training dataset is distinct from any publicly available shoplifting detection dataset in that it contains annotation categories for garment-type concealment specific to Indian subcontinental clothing conventions.
Claim 4 — Dependent: Low-Bandwidth Alert ›
Claim 4 (dependent on Claim 1): The method of Claim 1, wherein the step of transmitting the concealment event record further comprises: compressing the video clip to a file size not exceeding 5 megabytes using adaptive bitrate compression, and transmitting the compressed clip as a WhatsApp media message attachment alongside a structured text message comprising the concealment type classification, confidence score, store identifier, and timestamp, such that the total transmitted alert is deliverable over mobile internet connections with available bandwidth as low as 256 kilobits per second.
Claim 5 — Dependent: Repeat Offender ›
Claim 5 (dependent on Claim 1): The method of Claim 1, further comprising: maintaining a behavioral fingerprint database of persons detected performing concealment behaviors, wherein each fingerprint comprises extracted visual features of the person's appearance, and wherein upon detection of a subsequent concealment event by a person whose visual features substantially match an existing fingerprint, the alert notification transmitted via WhatsApp is supplemented with an indication that the detected person is a suspected repeat offender.
Novelty Statement
The novelty of the present invention lies in the combination of: (a) machine learning models trained on India-specific concealment behavior datasets covering Indian clothing types not addressed in prior art, (b) a preprocessing pipeline specifically designed for the image quality characteristics of Indian surveillance cameras (CP Plus, Hikvision India variants, Dahua India variants), and (c) an alert delivery mechanism via WhatsApp Business API with video evidence optimized for low-bandwidth Indian mobile internet conditions. No prior art has been identified that discloses this specific combination.

Patent 2 — Sub-3-Second Alert Pipeline

Application Reference: BGX-PAT-002 | Category: Distributed Systems / SaaS Architecture

Method and System for Guaranteed Sub-3-Second Retail Security Alert Delivery via Messaging APIs Over Low-Bandwidth Networks in Emerging Market Conditions

Field of Invention
The present invention relates to real-time alert delivery systems for retail security applications, and more particularly to a method and system that guarantees delivery of AI-detected security event alerts to retail store owners via consumer messaging APIs within a maximum latency of three seconds, even under the intermittent and low-bandwidth internet connectivity conditions characteristic of retail premises in Indian urban and semi-urban markets.
Background of the Invention
Retail security alert systems in developed markets typically assume reliable high-bandwidth internet connectivity (5+ Mbps upload). Indian retail premises, including small kirana stores, jewellery shops, and electronics outlets, commonly operate on internet connections with upload bandwidth between 1–5 Mbps, variable latency, and intermittent packet loss, due to their reliance on consumer-grade broadband or mobile hotspot connections.

Existing retail AI surveillance platforms fail to deliver actionable alerts within an operationally useful time window under such network conditions, as they require transmission of high-resolution video streams or rely on proprietary mobile applications that require persistent background connections. The present invention solves this problem through a novel pipeline that separates inference computation (edge or cloud) from alert payload construction and delivery, and uses the WhatsApp Business API as a delivery channel due to its widespread adoption (500M+ users) and delivery guarantee mechanisms in Indian networks.
Summary of the Invention
The present invention provides a method and system for delivering retail security event alerts in under three seconds comprising: (i) a distributed inference pipeline with adaptive edge-cloud workload partitioning based on real-time network bandwidth measurement, (ii) an alert payload minimization module that dynamically adjusts video clip resolution and duration based on available upload bandwidth, (iii) a multi-channel delivery failover mechanism using WhatsApp Business API as primary channel with SMS and push notification as fallback channels, and (iv) a delivery confirmation tracking module that escalates undelivered alerts within a predefined timeout.
Independent Claims
Claim 1 — Independent Method Claim ›
Claim 1: A computer-implemented method for delivering retail security event alerts within a guaranteed maximum latency, the method comprising:
  1. continuously measuring, by a network monitoring module, the available upload bandwidth of the internet connection at the retail premises;
  2. upon detection of a security event by an AI inference engine, constructing, by an alert payload construction module, an alert payload comprising a text description, a confidence score, a camera identifier, and optionally a video evidence clip, wherein the resolution and duration of the optional video evidence clip is dynamically selected from a predefined set of configurations based on the measured available upload bandwidth;
  3. transmitting, by an alert dispatch module, the constructed alert payload as a message via the WhatsApp Business API to a pre-registered mobile device, wherein the transmission is initiated within one second of security event detection;
  4. monitoring, by a delivery confirmation module, the delivery status of the transmitted WhatsApp message using WhatsApp Business API delivery receipts, wherein if a delivery confirmation is not received within a first timeout period of three seconds, the alert dispatch module retransmits the alert payload via an SMS gateway, and if delivery confirmation is not received within a second timeout period of seven seconds, the alert dispatch module further transmits the alert payload via a push notification service;
  5. logging, by a persistence module, the security event record, alert payload, delivery timestamp, delivery channel used, and delivery confirmation status in a multi-tenant database.
Claim 2 — Independent System Claim ›
Claim 2: A system for delivering retail security event alerts with a maximum end-to-end latency of three seconds, the system comprising:
  1. a network monitoring module configured to continuously measure upload bandwidth availability at a retail premises;
  2. an adaptive payload constructor configured to dynamically adjust video evidence clip parameters based on measured bandwidth;
  3. a primary alert dispatcher configured to transmit alerts via the WhatsApp Business API;
  4. a delivery confirmation monitor configured to receive and process WhatsApp delivery receipts; and
  5. a failover dispatcher configured to retransmit undelivered alerts via SMS and push notification channels based on escalating timeout thresholds.
Claim 3 — Dependent: Edge-Cloud Partitioning ›
Claim 3 (dependent on Claim 1): The method of Claim 1, wherein the AI inference engine operates in a hybrid edge-cloud architecture, wherein: (a) when the measured available upload bandwidth exceeds a first threshold, video frames are transmitted to a cloud inference server for AI analysis; (b) when the measured available upload bandwidth is below the first threshold, AI inference is performed locally on an edge computing device co-located with the retail premises surveillance system; and (c) the switching between edge and cloud inference is performed dynamically without interruption of security event detection.
Claim 4 — Dependent: Bandwidth Tiers ›
Claim 4 (dependent on Claim 1): The method of Claim 1, wherein the predefined set of alert payload configurations comprises at least three tiers: (a) a high-bandwidth tier transmitting a video clip of 10–15 seconds duration at 720p resolution when bandwidth exceeds 2 Mbps; (b) a medium-bandwidth tier transmitting a video clip of 5–8 seconds duration at 480p resolution when bandwidth is between 512 Kbps and 2 Mbps; and (c) a low-bandwidth tier transmitting a static image frame and text description only when bandwidth is below 512 Kbps.
Claim 5 — Dependent: Multi-tenant Isolation ›
Claim 5 (dependent on Claim 2): The system of Claim 2, wherein the persistence module implements a multi-tenant data architecture in which security event records, alert delivery logs, and configuration parameters of each retail store tenant are stored in logically isolated data partitions, and wherein a tenant administrator interface provides each retail store operator access exclusively to their own tenant's data without access to data of other tenants.
Novelty Statement
The novelty lies in the specific combination of: (a) dynamic alert payload construction tied to real-time bandwidth measurement, (b) use of WhatsApp Business API as the primary delivery channel with delivery receipt-based escalation, (c) the specific three-tier bandwidth adaptation model with defined thresholds suited for Indian retail network conditions, and (d) the multi-channel failover sequence (WhatsApp → SMS → Push) with specific timeout thresholds. No prior art discloses this specific combination for retail security applications in emerging market network conditions.
