## 1. Who to pitch first (priority order)

| Priority | Prospect | Why they say yes fast |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | **Independent jewellery shops** (gold/silver, single & family-run) | Highest per-item loss; owner is often present and emotionally invested |
| 🥈 | **Watch & luxury accessory boutiques** | High ticket items, small showcase footprint, try-on culture |
| 🥉 | **Premium bags / leather / eyewear stores** | Items are small, valuable, and handed across a counter |
| 4 | **Mobile & electronics showcases** | Demo units walk away; display tables are the "tray" |

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** high-value, small-form-factor inventory,
served over a counter by staff, existing IP cameras already installed, and
shrinkage the owner *feels* but cannot prove.

## 2. The 30-second pitch

**English:**
> "Your cameras watch the room — but nobody is watching the *counter*. The
> moment a hand dips into an open display tray, or someone grabs a piece and
> heads for the door, your phone should buzz *right then*, not the next
> morning. Retail Nazar's High-Value Counter does exactly that: it tracks the
> hand, finger by finger, and alerts you while the item is still in the shop.
> No shelves, no POS, no new cameras. Want me to show you in two minutes?"

**Hindi (short version):**
> "Aapke CCTV room dekhte hain, lekin *counter* par nazar koi nahi rakhta.
> Jaise hi koi haath tray ke andar daale, ya saaman leke door ki taraf bhaage —
> aapke phone par usi waqt alert aana chahiye, agle din nahi. Retail Nazar ka

## 3. Discovery questions (find the pain first)

1. "How do you currently hand over a piece to a customer — tray, case, or
   across the counter?"
2. "When was the last time something went missing, and when did you actually
   *notice*?"
3. "How many people can watch the counter while you're serving another
   customer?"
4. "If a hand went into the tray today, would you know within 10 seconds?"
5. "Do you have any cameras? Are they IP/network cameras or old analog?"
6. "What does one lost piece cost you — not just the price, but the margin you
   have to sell to recover it?"

*The goal: get them to say the counter is the vulnerable spot, and that they
find out about losses late.*

---

## 4. Live demo script (5 minutes, no real theft)

1. **Open the High-Value Counter page** in the dashboard.
2. Draw the zones on the live counter camera:
   - **Serving Counter** — the customer-facing surface.
   - **Display Tray** — the open tray where pieces are handed over.
   - **Display Case** — the glass case.
   - **Exit Door** — the door.
   - (Optional) **Approach Corridor** — the path to the counter.
3. **Save.**
4. Go to **Demo Center → "Fire High-Value Counter Demo"**.
5. Narrate the three alerts as they land in **Incidents**:
   - 🖐️ **Case Hand Reach** → "the hand went in and withdrew."
   - 💍 **Tray Change** → "the tray contents dropped."
   - 🏃 **Grab & Run** → "fast movement from the case to the exit."
6. Close: *"That alert reached your phone in under a second. Your current CCTV
   would have shown you this tomorrow."*

**Tip:** fire the three **individual cards** (Case Hand Reach / Tray Change /
Grab & Run) to show each signal separately before the full chain.

## 5. ROI math (make it concrete)

> **One prevented theft pays for the year.**

| Example | Value |
|---|---|
| One gold chain / watch lost | ₹50,000 – ₹3,00,000 |
| Retail Nazar Starter | ₹1,999 / month (~₹24,000 / year) |
| Annual plan (10+2 months) | ~₹1,666 / month effective |

**The line that lands:** *"If this stops one ₹50,000 piece from walking out
this year, you're already ₹26,000 ahead — and the system keeps guarding every
day after that."*

**Shrinkage frame:** typical retail shrinkage is **1.5–2.5% of turnover**. On a
₹1 crore counter business, that's ₹1.5–2.5 lakh/year quietly disappearing.
Retail Nazar costs a small fraction of that.

## 6. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have CCTV." | "CCTV is a witness, not a guard. It records the theft so you can file a report. We alert you *during* the theft, while the piece is still in the shop." |
| "Jewellery is handed over by staff — it's normal." | "Exactly — that's why shelf-AI doesn't work for you. We don't alarm every handover; we flag the *reach-in + withdraw + tray-change + fast-exit* pattern for *you* to confirm in real time." |
| "It will give too many false alarms." | "It's a review candidate, not an auto-arrest. You confirm each alert with a tap. And because it tracks fingertips, not just 'someone nearby', the signal is far more specific." |
| "We have no POS / barcode system." | "You don't need one. Detection is 100% visual — counter, tray, case, exit. Nothing to integrate." |
| "Too expensive." | "One prevented theft pays for the year. Starter is ₹1,999/month — less than a single silver chain." |
| "Installation will be painful." | "No new cameras. We connect to your existing IP cameras, and drawing the zones is point-and-click on the live view — 15 minutes." |
| "Privacy / cloud concerns." | "Video is analysed on-site at the edge. It doesn't need to leave your store." |
| "We'll think about it." | "Fair. Can I leave you with one number — how much your last missing piece cost? I'll call Thursday to show you the live demo on *your* camera." |

## 9. Do's & don'ts

**Do:**
- ✅ Demo live on *their* counter camera — the "aha" moment is seeing their own
  room with polygons drawn on it.
- ✅ Speak in loss numbers, not model names.
- ✅ Be honest that it's a review-candidate (this builds trust and matches how
  the product actually works).
- ✅ Reference the animated Help Center story + setup diagram as leave-behinds.

**Don't:**
- ❌ Claim it's 100% proof or that it "identifies thieves".
- ❌ Say it needs new cameras or a POS.
- ❌ Over-promise "zero false alarms" — say "specific, and you confirm in a tap".
- ❌ Pitch shelf-shoplifting features to a jeweller first — pitch the *counter*.

## 10. Closing & follow-up

1. End the demo with the **Incidents screen** showing the fired alerts — that is
   the product, not a slide.
2. Ask for **one specific date** to go live on their camera ("Can we turn it on
   for a 14-day trial starting Monday?").
3. Leave the **setup diagram** (`frontend/web/public/images/faq/high-value-counter-setup.svg`)
   and the **setup guide** (`docs/07_UserGuides/25_High_Value_Counter_Setup_Guide.md`)
   as the "what to expect" handout.
4. Follow up within 48 hours with the ROI number they gave you in discovery.

> **Final word:** jewellers don't lose stock off a shelf — they lose it at the
> counter. Sell the counter.
