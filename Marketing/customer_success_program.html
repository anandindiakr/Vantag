Churn Prevention Playbook

Save Rate Target: 40%

Warning Signals — Priority Order

🚨

CRITICAL: Payment failed + no response to reminder

Action within 2 hours. Personal call from CS rep. Check if account access issue.

🚨

CRITICAL: Support ticket open 48+ hours unresolved

Immediate escalation to Level 2. Customer must be contacted before resolution.

⚠️

HIGH: No login in 14 days

Personal WhatsApp within 24 hours. Offer setup optimization session.

⚠️

HIGH: NPS score below 6

Personal call within 2 hours. Find root cause. Escalate to founder if needed.

⚠️

MEDIUM: Camera disconnected 7+ days, no setup call

Proactive outreach. On-site setup offer.

💡

LOW: Only 1 of 3+ plan cameras connected after 14 days

Feature education. Offer "camera setup day" for unused cameras.

💡

LOW: "Forward to owner" language in WhatsApp

Decision maker not engaged. Request direct intro to owner.

Cancellation Save Protocol

NEVER process cancellation immediately.

ALWAYS say: "Ek kaam karein — aap cancel karne se pehle 2 minute baat kar lete hain? Main personally ensure karna chahta hoon ki koi problem hai toh fix ho sake."

Save Offer Hierarchy (use in this order):

  1. Free setup optimization session — remove friction first
  2. Downgrade to lower plan (not cancel) — preserve relationship
  3. 1-month pause — "We'll keep your data safe"
  4. 50% off next 2 months — value reaffirmation
  5. Founder call — last resort for high-value (₹3,499+ plan) accounts

Support Escalation Matrix

3-Tier System

Level 1 — CS Rep

Resolve within: 2 hours

  • Camera setup help
  • Alert configuration
  • App/dashboard navigation
  • Password reset
  • Billing queries
  • WhatsApp alert setup

Level 2 — Senior CS / Tech

Resolve within: 8 hours

  • Camera won't connect after troubleshooting
  • Missing historical footage
  • False alert rate too high
  • Performance issues (slow)
  • Multi-location setup issues

Level 3 — Engineering

Resolve within: 24 hours

  • Data breach or security concern
  • Platform outage affecting customer
  • AI detection incorrect after calibration
  • Integration issues
  • Database / account data issues

Escalation Rules

L1 → L2 triggers:Unresolved after 2 hrs OR customer unhappy with response
L2 → L3 triggers:Technical issue confirmed + unresolved after 4 hours
Any → Founder:Customer threatens public complaint, legal action, OR ARPU > ₹5,000/month
Max response time:No customer should wait more than 24 hrs for any response
