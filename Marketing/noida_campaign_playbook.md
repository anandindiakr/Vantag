# Retail Nazar — Noida Launch Campaign Playbook

## Overview
**Phase 1**: Noida (Sectors 18, 62, 63, NSEZ, Atta Market, Great India Place area)

---

## UNIQUE CAMPAIGN IDEAS

### 1. "CCTV to Smart" Campaign
**Concept**: "Your existing CCTV cameras are dumb. We make them smart — in 30 minutes, for free."
- Most shops in Noida already have CCTV (IP cameras)
- Retail Nazar plugs into existing cameras — no hardware purchase
- **Hook**: "You already paid for cameras. Now make them actually work."
- **Offer**: First month free if they let us put a small "Powered by Retail Nazar" sticker on their counter

### 2. "Nazar Rakhein" (Keep an Eye) Micro-Campaign
**Concept**: Play on the brand name — "Nazar" means both watch/eye and an Indian concept of protection
- Run in local WhatsApp groups for shopkeepers
- Message: "Dukaan pe Nazar rakhna ab aasaan hai"
- Use the "evil eye" (nazar) cultural metaphor — protection from theft

### 3. "Dukaan Bhi, Ghar Bhi" (Shop AND Home)
**Concept**: Target the shop owner who can't be at shop and home at same time
- Pain point: Owner goes home for lunch — staff slacks, pilferage happens
- Solution: Watch both places from one phone
- WhatsApp campaign around lunch hours (12–2 PM) specifically

### 4. ROI Calculator Challenge
**Concept**: "Tell us your monthly turnover. We'll calculate how much you're losing to shrinkage."
- Average 2% shrinkage = ₹20,000/month loss on ₹10L turnover
- Retail Nazar at ₹1,999/month = saves ₹18,000/month net
- Create a simple WhatsApp bot or Google Form for this

### 5. "Noida 100" Founding Member Program
**Concept**: Create exclusivity — "We are onboarding only 100 stores in Noida"
- Founding members get: 50% off first 3 months + their name/logo on our "Noida Partners" page
- Creates FOMO, urgency, community
- Counter: "87 of 100 spots taken" (update weekly on handouts/WhatsApp)

### 6. Neighbourhood Association Tie-up
**Target**: Sector 18 Traders' Association, Atta Market Vyapaar Mandal, etc.
- One association demo → word spreads within the community
- Offer: 20% referral discount for every member who refers another member
- Hindi tagline: "Pados ki dukan bhi Retail Nazar se protected hai?"

## WHATSAPP GROUP STRATEGY

### Target Groups to Join / Message
1. **Sector-wise Traders Groups** — Search on WhatsApp: "Sector 18 Noida Traders", "Sector 62 Business"
2. **Local Housing Society Groups** — Market areas near societies
3. **RWA Secretary contacts** — Many run market associations
4. **Atta Market Shopkeepers** — Busy market, high theft rate
5. **Electronic/Mobile Market groups** — High-value items = high theft concern

### Rules to Follow
- Never spam — max 1 message per group per week
- Always offer VALUE first (tip about retail security, etc.)
- Use local language (Hindi preferred in Noida NCR)
- Pin a success story — not a sales pitch
- Offer to come "free of cost" for a live demo

## INTRODUCTORY PRICING IDEAS FOR WEBSITE

### Option A — "Noida Founding 100" (Recommended)
**Mechanism**: Counter on website — "87/100 slots remaining"
- Growth plan: ₹1,999 → ₹999/month for first 3 months
- Shows urgency + exclusivity
- After 100 stores: revert to full price

### Option B — "Catch Your First Thief Free"
**Mechanism**: 7-day extended trial (vs normal 3 days) for first sign-up
- Tagline: "If we don't detect a single incident in 7 days, we extend free for another 7."
- High-confidence hook — shows the product works

### Option C — "ROI Guarantee"
**Mechanism**: "If Retail Nazar doesn't save you more than its cost in Month 1, we refund."
- Powerful trust builder for skeptical Indian market
- Clause: Must have at least 1 camera connected and operational

### Option D — "Refer & Earn"
**Mechanism**: Existing customer refers → gets 1 month free; New customer gets 50% off first month
- Pure word-of-mouth engine in tight shopkeeper communities
- Hindi tagline: "Dost ko Retail Nazar bata, ek mahina muft pa"

### Option E — "Annual = 2 Months Free"
**Mechanism**: Pay for 10 months, get 12
- ₹1,999 × 10 = ₹19,990 for full year (saves ₹3,998)
- Improves cash flow and reduces churn
