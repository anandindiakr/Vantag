2 Partner Tiers and Benefits
2.1 Tier Structure. BGXT operates a tiered partnership program. The applicable tier for the Partner is as selected in the Partnership Type checkbox above and confirmed in Schedule A. The benefits, obligations, and commission rates for each tier are set out below and in Schedule A.
Tier Commission / Revenue Share Minimum Active Subscribers Key Benefits
Referral Partner 10% of first-year MRR per referred customer None Referral link, basic marketing kit
Authorized Reseller 20% recurring revenue share 5 active subscribers Reseller portal, co-branded materials, priority support
System Integrator 25% recurring + co-branding rights 10 active subscribers/quarter API access, co-branding, BGXT certification, dedicated account manager
OEM / White-Label Custom pricing (Schedule A) 25 subscribers/quarter Full white-label, custom domain, Tier 1 support responsibility, White-Label Addendum
2.2 Referral Partner. A Referral Partner earns a commission of ten percent (10%) of the Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) generated from each customer referred by the Partner and successfully onboarded by BGXT, for the first twelve (12) months of such customer's subscription. No minimum subscriber obligation applies. Referral Partners have no resale, integration, or white-label rights.
2.3 Authorized Reseller. An Authorized Reseller earns a recurring revenue share of twenty percent (20%) on the net subscription fees of all customers brought by the Partner and maintained as active subscribers. The Partner must maintain a minimum of five (5) active paying subscribers at all times during the term. Failure to maintain this minimum for two (2) consecutive quarters may result in tier downgrade or termination as per Clause 9.
2.4 System Integrator Partner. A System Integrator Partner earns a recurring revenue share of twenty-five percent (25%) on net subscription fees, along with co-branding rights as specified in Clause 10. The Partner must:
2.4.1 Complete BGXT's mandatory product certification program (online, approximately four (4) hours) within sixty (60) days of the Effective Date;
2.4.2 Maintain a minimum of ten (10) active paying subscribers per quarter; and
2.4.3 Participate in quarterly business review meetings with BGXT as specified in Clause 9.3.
2.5 OEM / White-Label Partner. An OEM / White-Label Partner operates under custom pricing terms negotiated separately and documented in Schedule A and a separate White-Label Addendum (the "White-Label Addendum"), which shall form part of this Agreement upon execution. The Partner must maintain a minimum of twenty-five (25) active subscribers per quarter. OEM-specific terms are further detailed in Clause 5.
2.6 Tier Upgrades and Downgrades. BGXT may, at its sole discretion, upgrade the Partner's tier upon consistent outperformance of targets. BGXT may downgrade the Partner's tier upon failure to meet minimum subscriber obligations for two (2) consecutive quarters, with thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Tier changes shall take effect from the beginning of the next calendar quarter.
3 Commission Structure and Payment
3.1 Commission Basis. All commissions and revenue shares payable under this Agreement shall be calculated on the net subscription fee actually received by BGXT from the end customer, which shall exclude:
3.1.1 Goods and Services Tax (GST) and any other applicable taxes or levies;
3.1.2 Payment gateway fees, transaction charges, and bank charges;
3.1.3 Refunds, credits, or adjustments issued to the end customer; and
3.1.4 Any discounts approved by BGXT in writing.
3.2 Payment Schedule. BGXT shall calculate and pay commissions on a monthly basis. Commission statements shall be generated by the 10th day of each calendar month for the preceding month's eligible revenue, and payment shall be made by the 15th day of each calendar month via bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) to the Partner's designated bank account.
3.3 Minimum Payout Threshold. If the total commission payable to the Partner in any given month is less than Rupees Five Thousand (₹5,000), BGXT shall not be obligated to make payment in that month. Such amount shall be accumulated and carried forward to the following month(s) until the cumulative amount reaches or exceeds the threshold, at which point it shall be paid in the next regular payment cycle.
3.4 Commission on Upgrades. Where an end customer upgrades their subscription plan, the Partner shall earn commission on the incremental revenue attributable to the upgrade, calculated on a pro-rata basis for the remainder of the billing cycle, and on the full upgraded subscription fee from the next billing cycle onwards.
3.5 Chargeback and Clawback. If an end customer cancels their subscription or requests a refund within sixty (60) days of the subscription commencement date (the "Chargeback Period"), any commission paid to the Partner in respect of such customer shall be subject to clawback. BGXT shall deduct the clawed-back amount from the Partner's next commission payment. If no future commission is payable, the Partner shall repay the clawed-back amount within thirty (30) days of BGXT's written demand.
3.6 Tax Deduction at Source (TDS). All commission payments shall be subject to deduction of Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as applicable under the Income Tax Act, 1961, and any other applicable Indian tax laws. BGXT shall issue TDS certificates (Form 16A) to the Partner within the timelines prescribed by law. The Partner shall provide its PAN and other tax details as required by BGXT.
3.7 GST Compliance. The Partner shall be responsible for its own GST registration and compliance obligations. Where applicable, the Partner shall issue a valid GST invoice to BGXT for commission amounts, and BGXT shall make payment against such invoice. The Partner warrants that it shall comply with all applicable GST laws and regulations.
3.8 Dispute of Commission Statements. The Partner must raise any dispute regarding a commission statement within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Failure to raise a dispute within this period shall be deemed acceptance of the statement. Disputed amounts shall be resolved in good faith within thirty (30) days of the dispute being raised.
3.9 No Commission on Direct Sales. The Partner shall not be entitled to any commission on customers who approach BGXT directly, customers who were already in BGXT's CRM prior to the Partner's introduction, or customers introduced by another partner, unless BGXT expressly agrees otherwise in writing.
