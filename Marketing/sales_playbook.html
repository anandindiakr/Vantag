MEDDIC Qualification Questions

M — Metric: Quantify the Pain▼
"Pichle 6 mahine mein stock audit mein kitna difference aaya?"
"How much stock loss have you seen in the last 6 months?"

👉 If they say "nothing" — they're not tracking. That's also a problem you can solve.

E — Economic Buyer: Right Person?▼
"Security ya surveillance ke liye aap hi decide karte hain?"
"Are you the one who decides on security spending here?"

👉 If no — ask for the owner. Never pitch to a manager without owner present.

I — Identify Pain: The Silent Moment▼
"Abhi, agar aapki dukaan mein kuch galat ho raha ho, toh aapko kaise pata chalega?"
"Right now, if something wrong was happening in your store, how would you know?"

👉 Stay silent after this question. Let them feel the gap. That silence is your best selling tool.

Stage 1: Approach & Opening (First 60 Seconds)▼

You have 60 seconds before they ask you to leave. Lead with curiosity, not a pitch.

The Permission Opener (Best for cold visits):

"Namaste [Name] ji, main Retail Nazar se hoon. Ek AI product hai jo aapke existing CCTV cameras ko smart bana deta hai — theft ka WhatsApp alert aata hai 3 second mein. Kya aap 2 minute de sakte hain?"
"Good morning — I'm from Retail Nazar. We help store owners catch theft in real-time using their existing CCTV. AI sends a WhatsApp alert in 3 seconds. Can I show you something in 2 minutes?"

NEVER SAY
  • "Mujhe aapko kuch bechna hai" — sounds pushy immediately
  • "Kya aap security mein interested hain?" — too generic
  • Start with price before showing value
  • Stand awkwardly while owner serves another customer — come back
Stage 2: Discovery Questions▼

Ask conversationally — not like a checklist:

  1. "Aapki dukaan mein CCTV hai?" — If yes: great. If no: ask why not.
  2. "Kabhi stock audit mein difference aaya ho?" — Opens pain conversation.
  3. "Aap khud kitne ghante dukaan par rehte hain?" — Reveals monitoring gap.
  4. "Jab aap nahi hote, koi chinta hoti hai?" — Emotional trigger.
  5. "Kya koi incident hua jisme shak tha lekin proof nahi mila?" — The golden question.
  6. "Abhi agar kuch galat ho raha ho, aapko kaise pata chalega?" — THEN STAY SILENT.

Objection Handling Matrix (20 Objections)

Most Critical ▼
💰 Price "₹999 bhi bahut zyada hai" / "Too expensive"▼
NEVER discount immediately.

"Bilkul samajh mein aata hai. Ek cheez bataiye — pichle mahine stock mein kitna farak aaya? [Pause] Agar sirf ₹1,000 ka bhi hua, toh Retail Nazar ne pehle din hi apna paisa wapas kar diya. Aur zyada chance hai ki ₹5,000–10,000 ka hua hoga."

Then bridge to trial: "Isiliye 3 din free dete hain. Kuch invest mat karo — sirf try karo."

💰 Price "Abhi budget nahi hai"▼
"Bilkul. Budget kab aata hai? [Listen]. Tab tak ke liye — abhi 3 din free try karo. Paisa kuch nahi lagta. Tab decide karna."
💰 Price "Market mein sasta option milega"▼
"Ho sakta hai. Lekin woh options AI-based hain ya manual monitoring? Koi baith ke camera dekh raha hai, toh ₹999 mein bhi unka kharcha hai. Hamara AI 24/7 kaam karta hai — koi nahi thakta, koi nahi sohta. Functionality ka comparison zaroor karo."
🔒 Trust "Naya product hai, kya pata kaam kare ya nahi"▼
"Sahi sawaal hai — exactly isliye 3-din free trial deta hoon. Koi agar aise kehta toh main kehta: try karo pehle. 3 din mein agar ek bhi useful alert nahi aaya, toh chhod do. Zero investment. Par agar kaam kiya toh aapko khud samajh aa jayega."

ADD "Hum BrainGuardX AI Technologies ke product hain — registered Indian company. 23+ stores already Noida mein hain."
🔒 Trust "Abhi time nahi hai — kal aana"▼
"Zaroor. Kab best time hoga? [Get specific date+time]"

Always leave with WhatsApp contact. Never leave empty-handed.

"Tab tak ke liye — kya main ek WhatsApp message bhej sakta hoon with our website link? Kabhi free mein dekh lena."
🔒 Trust "Partner/CA se poochhna hai pehle"▼
"Bilkul sahi approach hai. Kya unhe abhi call kar sakte hain? Main wait karta hoon. Ya best — kya hum ek 15-minute call set kar sakte hain jab aap dono available ho?"

Offer: "Unka number dete hain toh main directly explain kar deta hoon."

🔒 Trust "Online payment par trust nahi"▼
"Bilkul samjhaa. Isliye hum Razorpay use karte hain — same jo Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon use karta hai. Aur trial ke liye toh payment hi nahi hota — sirf email se register karo. Try karo pehle, paisa baad mein."
🔧 Tech "Mera internet slow hai"▼
"Minimum 2 Mbps upload chahiye per camera. Kya hum abhi check kar sakte hain? [speedtest.net]. Agar speed kam bhi hai, 2–3 cameras connect karo — woh easily kaam karega."
🔧 Tech "Meri Hikvision/Dahua camera hai — kaam karega?"▼
"Haan bilkul — Hikvision aur Dahua dono supported hain. India ke 80% IP cameras ke saath kaam karta hai. Model number bataiye — main 30 second mein confirm kar deta hoon."
TIP Always carry a camera compatibility list.
🔧 Tech "Data kahan store hota hai? Safe hai?"▼
"Aapka data India ke cloud servers pe store hota hai — encrypted. Koi bhi dekh nahi sakta — na hum, na koi third party. DPDP Act 2023 ke compliance mein hain — India ka new data protection law."
🔧 Tech "Bahut complicated lagta hai / Technical nahi hoon"▼
"Main samjhaa — exactly isliye humne isko simple banaya hai. Setup mein sirf ek kaam hai — apna camera ka IP address dena. Main khud aapke saath baith ke 30 minute mein set up kar dunga — pehle din free on-site support milta hai."
🏆 Comp "Already ek security guard hai"▼
"Bahut accha — guard physical deterrence ke liye important hai. Lekin guard soh bhi sakta hai, distracted bhi ho sakta hai. Retail Nazar guard ki aankhon ko AI ka support deta hai — dono milke zyada effective hain. Aur ek cheez — agar guard khud andar ka banda hai?"
🏆 Comp "Mera bhai manually camera dekh leta hai"▼
"Accha setup hai! Lekin aapka bhai poora din 100% time dekh sakta hai? AI ek second bhi nahi chukta. Aur agar kuch ho toh bhai ko automatically alert jaata hai — manually dekhne ki zaroorat hi nahi."
🏆 Comp "Pehle CCTV company se baat karunga"▼
"Bilkul sahi karte hain. Lekin yeh dono alag cheezein hain — CCTV company hardware deti hai, hum usse intelligent banaate hain. Bahut CCTV dealers humare saath partner hain — agar dealer ka number dete hain toh main unse baat kar sakta hoon."
💛 Internal "Mujhe apne staff pe trust hai"▼
"Bahut achchi baat hai — trust important hai. Main yeh nahi keh raha ki aapka staff bura hai. Main keh raha hoon ki 70% internal theft wahan hoti hai jahan owner ko poora bharosa tha. Retail Nazar trust ki jagah nahi leta — woh proof deta hai jab kuch galat ho. Agar staff honest hai, toh aapko kabhi alert hi nahi aayega."
💛 Internal "Pehle koi iske baare mein nahi suna"▼
"Bilkul sahi — hum Noida mein abhi launch ho rahe hain. Isiliye founding members ko 50% discount de rahe hain — sirf pehle 100 stores ko. Agar aap 6 mahine wait karoge, yeh offer nahi rahega. Early adopters ko hamesha faayda hota hai."
⏰ Stall "Sochna hai" / "Let me think about it"▼
NEVER SAY "Sure, take your time" and walk away.

"Bilkul. Kya main pooch sakta hoon — kya specifically soch rahe hain? Price ke baare mein, ya product ke baare mein? [Listen]. Accha, toh yeh karte hain — abhi sirf free trial ke liye register karo. Sochna baad mein bhi karte rehna — trial mein kuch kharcha nahi."
⏰ Stall "Kal aana" / "Come back tomorrow"▼
"Zaroor. Lekin ek cheez — kya hum abhi bas email se register kar lete hain free trial ke liye? Kal jab aayunga tab saath mein setup bhi kar denge. Sirf email chahiye — 30 second ka kaam hai."
⏰ Stall "Nahin chahiye" (Flat No)▼
"Bilkul theek hai — main force nahi karunga. Ek kaam karein — kya main ek WhatsApp message bhej sakta hoon? Kabhi bhi zaroorat pade, link pe click karna. 3 din ke liye free hai — jab bhi mann kare try karna."

The "no" today is "maybe" in 3 months. Always leave the door open.

TRIAL CLOSE — Best for Undecided Customers

"Chaliye yeh karte hain — abhi sirf 3-din ka trial start karte hain. Koi paisa nahi, koi commitment nahi. Agar kaam kiya toh baaat karte hain. Deal?"

Use when: Customer is interested but hesitating to commit.

2

ROI CLOSE — For Price Objectors

"Ek simple calculation karte hain. Aapka monthly turnover roughly kitna hai? [₹X]. Retail industry average 1.5–2% shrinkage hoti hai. Toh aap roughly ₹[X×0.015] per month kho rahe hain. Retail Nazar ₹999 mein aata hai — yeh toh ek investment hai."
TIP Write this on paper — visual impact multiplies conviction.
3

URGENCY CLOSE — For Stalling Customers

"Noida mein pehle 100 founding stores ko 50% off milta hai — abhi sirf [X] jagah bacha hai. Aaj register karoge toh yeh rate lock ho jaata hai aapke liye."
ONLY USE when the offer is genuinely limited — never fake urgency.
4

SOCIAL PROOF CLOSE — For Trust Objectors

"Ek kaam karo — main aapko [nearby store] ka number deta hoon. Unhe directly call karo aur poochho unka experience kaisa raha. Uske baad decide karo."
PREP Have 2–3 willing reference customers ready per area.
5

ASSUMPTIVE CLOSE — For Warm Leads

"Aapke paas [X] cameras hain — Growth plan perfect rahega. Chalo main abhi registration form bhar deta hoon — bas aapka email chahiye."
ONLY USE when you sense strong positive buying signals.
6

QUESTION CLOSE — Flushes Out Final Objection

"Mujhe ek baat bataiye — agar pricing aur technical sab theek ho, toh kya koi reason hai jo aapko rokta hai aaj start karne se?"

Forces commitment or reveals the REAL objection you haven't addressed yet.

Do's & Don'ts

Golden Rules ▼

ALWAYS DO

  • Lead with a question, not a pitch
  • Get WhatsApp number even if they say no
  • Leave a physical handout with QR code
  • Follow up exactly when you said you would
  • Know the nearest reference customer in that area
  • Speak their language (Hindi in most Noida markets)
  • Offer to do setup WITH them, not for them
  • Know your product specs cold (camera models, speed req)
  • Always ask "why" when they say no — there's a real objection
  • Celebrate when they get their first alert

NEVER DO

  • Badmouth competitors — ever
  • Promise features Retail Nazar doesn't have
  • Discount without manager approval
  • Pressure someone after a hard no twice
  • Leave without getting contact info
  • Exaggerate detection accuracy
  • Make legal claims (e.g., "FIR-proof evidence")
  • Show more than 3 features in a first demo
  • Ignore an objection and keep pitching
  • Arrive during peak rush hours without an appointment
